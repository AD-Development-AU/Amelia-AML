Start

This assistant walks a small legal practice through customising AUSTRAC's Legal profession program starter kit. It collects practice, governance, service, client, risk and CDD inputs, recommends the relevant documents from the AUSTRAC library, and generates downloadable DOCX outputs from your own templates.

Product boundary. This is a starter-kit document assistant. It does not decide compliance, verify customer identity, screen sanctions (DFAT consolidated list), screen PEPs, monitor transactions, file SMRs/TTRs/IFTIs, or enrol the practice with AUSTRAC. Those actions remain the practice's responsibility and are performed via AUSTRAC Online and the practice's own identity-verification, sanctions and monitoring controls.
Key dates (Tranche 2 commencement). AUSTRAC Online enrolment opens 31 March 2026. AML/CTF obligations bite from 1 July 2026 — a legal practice must have an AML/CTF program in place before providing any designated service from that date. Enrolment with AUSTRAC and appointment of an AML/CTF Compliance Officer are required within 28 days of first providing a designated service. Independent evaluation of the entire program is required at least every 3 years; first one falls 30 June 2029–2030 under staggered transitional rules.

Suitability self-assessment

AUSTRAC's starter kit is designed only for practices that meet all of the criteria below. If your practice does not meet a criterion, you may still use the kit but you must record why the kit is suitable for your circumstances or customise the program more deeply than this assistant assumes.

Practice profile

Starter kit is targeted at practices with 15 or fewer.

Governance & personnel

AUSTRAC requires three distinct roles: governing body (overall oversight), senior manager (approves the program and material decisions; cannot delegate), and AML/CTF Compliance Officer (day-to-day; management-level; fit and proper; Australian resident if the practice has a permanent Australian establishment). For one-person practices the same person typically fills all three.

Reporting, recordkeeping & training

Tipping-off (AML/CTF Act s 123). Forms generated from this step, and the SMR/escalation forms recommended in Step 10, may contain reportable-matter detail and customer-identifying information. Do not share these forms with the customer or any other person without a need to know. Tipping-off is a criminal offence carrying up to two years imprisonment. Store outputs on encrypted media, transmit only via secure channels, and continue ongoing CDD without tipping off.
