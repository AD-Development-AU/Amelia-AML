# Disclaimer

**Not legal advice. Not affiliated with AUSTRAC. Use at your own risk.**

## What this tool is

The Legal AML Starter Kit Assistant is a free, open-source document-assembly
aid. It helps an Australian legal practice prepare records from its own inputs,
structured around AUSTRAC's publicly-available Legal profession program starter
kit (January 2026 release).

## What this tool is not

This tool does **not**:

- Provide legal advice. Nothing it outputs is a legal opinion.
- Determine whether your practice is compliant with the *Anti-Money Laundering
  and Counter-Terrorism Financing Act 2006* (Cth), the AML/CTF Rules 2025, or
  any other law.
- Verify customer identity, screen for sanctioned persons (e.g. the DFAT
  Consolidated List), screen for politically exposed persons, or monitor
  transactions.
- Lodge suspicious matter reports (SMRs), threshold transaction reports (TTRs),
  international funds transfer instruction reports (IFTIs), or annual
  compliance reports with AUSTRAC.
- Enrol your practice with AUSTRAC Online.
- Replace independent legal advice or independent program evaluation.

## Your responsibilities

If you use this tool, you remain responsible for:

- Determining whether your practice is required to comply with AML/CTF law and,
  if so, the scope of that obligation.
- Customising the records this tool helps produce so they reflect your
  practice's actual circumstances, risk profile and controls.
- Having the records reviewed by a person with appropriate AML/CTF expertise
  before relying on them.
- Performing customer due diligence, sanctions and PEP screening, transaction
  monitoring, reporting, recordkeeping and training in line with your AML/CTF
  obligations.
- Enrolment with AUSTRAC, appointment and notification of an AML/CTF Compliance
  Officer, and all other regulator-facing actions.
- Securing any client information stored locally on your device or exported as
  JSON, in line with the *Privacy Act 1988* (Cth) and your professional
  obligations of confidentiality.

## Defaults and pre-populated content

This tool ships with default values reflecting the AML/CTF Rules 2025 as at the
date stamped in the source. If a Rule changes after that date, the defaults may
become incorrect. Treat every default as a starting point you have verified
against the current law, not a settled answer.

## Tipping-off

Some forms this tool helps prepare contain information that may be reportable
to AUSTRAC, including detail that identifies a customer. Sharing this detail
with the customer is a criminal offence under s 123 of the *Anti-Money
Laundering and Counter-Terrorism Financing Act 2006* (Cth), with a maximum
penalty of two years imprisonment. Store outputs securely. Do not transmit
unencrypted. Restrict access on a need-to-know basis.
